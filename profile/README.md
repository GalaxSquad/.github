# Galaxy Apps — Shopify App Studio

**We build Shopify apps that protect your store automatically.**

Galaxy Apps is a small product team based in Hanoi, Vietnam. We design, engineer, and maintain the **ThemeSafe** and **OrderSafe** app suite on the Shopify App Store — automated backups and AI-powered theme security for merchants worldwide. No agency work, no custom store builds: we only build and support our own products.

## Our apps

| App | What it does | On the Shopify App Store |
|---|---|---|
| **ThemeSafe – Security – Review** ⭐ 5.0 (7 reviews) | AI-powered security scanning for Shopify themes. Combines rule-based detection with AI analysis to flag suspicious scripts, injected code, and unexpected theme changes — with real-time monitoring and alerts. Free / $19 / $49 plans. | [View app](https://apps.shopify.com/themesafe-security) |
| **ThemeSafe – Auto Backup** (live since Nov 2023) | Scheduled automatic backups of themes, templates, sections, and images, with one-click restore and Google Drive / Dropbox sync. | [View app](https://apps.shopify.com/theme-backup-1) |
| **OrderSafe – Auto Backup** ⭐ 5.0 (1 review) | Hands-free archiving of every new Shopify order to email and cloud storage for audit and recovery. | [View app](https://apps.shopify.com/ordersafe-auto-backup) |

## What we focus on

- **AI in the product, not just the pitch** — ThemeSafe Security reviews merchants’ actual theme code through the Shopify API, pairing deterministic static rules with contextual AI analysis.
- **Automation by default** — backups, scans, and monitoring run in the background via Shopify APIs, with zero scripts injected into the storefront.
- **Direct engineering support** — every support ticket is answered by the people who build the apps.
